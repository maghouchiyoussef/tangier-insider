It's the most common mistake visitors make: they book a cheap ferry "to Tangier" and only realise on arrival that they've landed at **Tanger Med**, a big industrial port outside the city. Here's how the two ports compare.

## The short answer

- **Visiting Tangier?** Take the ferry from **Tarifa** to **Tangier Ville**. You walk off the boat in the city.
- **Driving further into Morocco?** **Algeciras → Tanger Med** often makes sense: more departures, and you join the motorway directly.

## Side by side

<div class="table-scroll">

| | Tangier Ville | Tanger Med |
|---|---|---|
| Where | In the city, between the old medina and the new town | Outside the city, 35–54 km away depending on the district |
| Ferry from | **Tarifa** | **Algeciras** |
| Crossing time | About **1 hour** | About **1.5 hours** (AML lists 2 hours) |
| Companies | Baleària, AML | Baleària, AML, DFDS, Trasmediterránea |
| Foot passenger, one way* | Baleària **41 €**, AML **317 MAD** | DFDS **31 €**, Baleària **36 €**, AML **224 MAD** |
| Getting into Tangier | Walk (less than 15 min) or petit taxi | Taxi, bus or shuttle, about 20–30 min by road |

</div>

\*Prices checked on the operators' official websites on 22 November 2026, for a weekday in December 2026. They change with the season: always check the live price.

## Why Tanger Med can look cheaper

The Algeciras–Tanger Med ticket is often a few euros cheaper. But once you add the trip from Tanger Med into Tangier, and the extra time, the saving usually disappears if Tangier is your destination.

## Getting from Tanger Med to Tangier

According to the [official Tanger Med passenger website](https://www.tangermed-passagers.com/fr/mon-voyage/depuis-tanger-med), you can continue by **taxi**, the **ALSA** bus or a shuttle. The drive takes about **20–30 minutes**. Prices are not published there, so agree on any taxi fare before you set off.

From experience, here are your real options:

- **A private taxi ("course")**: the quickest. Negotiate the price before you get in; around **100 dirhams** is a reasonable maximum to Tangier.
- **A regional grand taxi**: shared taxis passing through from Fnideq or Dalia sometimes have a free seat. If one does, it's cheaper.
- **The bus**: the cheapest, around **8 dirhams** (local estimate). You need to walk to the bus stop outside the port.

Buses and shared grand taxis usually drop you near **Tangier Ville train station**, about 20 minutes by petit taxi from the city centre (see the [Al Boraq guide](/tangier-train-al-boraq/)).

**Local tip:** if the taxi price at the port is too high, be patient: walk out and take the bus or a shared taxi instead.

## Passport control

On both routes, Moroccan passport control usually happens **on board** during the crossing. Get in the queue as soon as the boat leaves. More details in the [Tarifa–Tangier ferry guide](/ferry-tarifa-tangier/).
