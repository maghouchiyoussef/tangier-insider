One day is enough to get a real feel for Tangier if you keep it simple and walk. This plan works whether you arrive by ferry from Tarifa, by train, or you're staying in town.

## Morning: the medina and the Kasbah

1. **Grand Socco**: start at the busy square at the entrance of the medina.
2. **The medina**: wander the narrow streets towards the **Petit Socco**. Some alleys are dead ends, so keep an offline map handy.
3. **The Kasbah**: walk up to the walled upper town for the views over the Strait. The [Kasbah Museum](/things-to-do-in-tangier/) is open 10:00–18:00 and usually closed on Tuesdays.

**Arriving by ferry?** If you only have a small backpack, just walk: the city port sits right between the old medina and the new town, less than 15 minutes on foot from both. With heavy luggage, drop your bags at your hotel first (take a petit taxi if needed): the medina is much easier to explore with free hands. See the [ferry guide](/ferry-tarifa-tangier/).

## Lunch

You'll find restaurants both in the **old medina** and in the **new town**. Go for a traditional Moroccan restaurant and try a **tagine**, **couscous** or **lamb with prunes** (*lham b'barkouk*). If you just want something quick, there are plenty of fast-food places too.

## Afternoon: Café Hafa and the Marshan

From the Kasbah, it's an easy walk to the **Marshan** neighbourhood and **Café Hafa**. Stop there for mint tea on the terraces above the sea. In the afternoon it gets busy, so if you prefer it calm, swap this stop for the morning.

**Short on energy?** The red [City Tour Tanger](https://tanger.city-tour.com/en/) hop-on hop-off bus loops around the city and goes out to **Cap Spartel**.

## Sunset: Merkala

Head down to the **Merkala seafront**, where locals stroll and watch the sun go down over the Strait. If you enjoy walking, go on foot; otherwise a petit taxi gets you there quickly.

## Evening: back to the Petit Socco

End the day at the **Petit Socco**, lively in the evening with its cafés.
