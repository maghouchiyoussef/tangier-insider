Crossing from Spain to Tangier is one of the easiest ways to reach Morocco: the shortest route takes about an hour, and the ferry lands right next to the old town. Here is everything you need to know, from someone who lives at the other end of the crossing.

## Which route should you take?

There are two main crossings to Tangier from the south of Spain. They arrive at **two different ports**, and this is the most common mistake visitors make.

**If you are visiting Tangier itself, take the Tarifa ferry.** You walk off the boat and you are a few minutes from the medina. From Tanger Med you still need a bus or taxi of roughly an hour to reach the city [VERIFY].

## Passport control happens on board

On the Tarifa–Tangier crossing, Moroccan passport control usually takes place **during the crossing**, at a desk inside the ferry [VERIFY]. As soon as the boat leaves, look for the queue and get your passport stamped. If you wait until the end, you may be stuck in a long line while everyone else walks off.

## Mind the time difference

Morocco and Spain are not always on the same time. Spain changes to summer time; Morocco does not follow the same rules, and Morocco also changes its clock during Ramadan [VERIFY the current rules]. Check the departure time **in the time zone of the port you leave from**, and add a margin.

## From the port to the medina

From Tangier Ville port you can:

- **Walk**: the medina is a short walk uphill from the port exit [VERIFY time].
- **Take a petit taxi** (small blue taxi): ask the driver to use the meter, or agree a price before you get in [VERIFY typical fare].

Expect people offering to guide you as you leave the port. A polite "la, shukran" (no, thank you) and walking on with purpose is usually enough. See [Is Tangier safe?](/is-tangier-safe/) for more on unofficial guides.

## Money

The Moroccan dirham can't easily be bought outside Morocco. Use an ATM after arrival, or change a small amount at the port or a bank in town [VERIFY exchange options at the port]. Many small shops and taxis only take cash.

## Day trip from Tarifa: a simple plan

1. Morning ferry from Tarifa, passport stamped on board.
2. Walk up to the medina and the Kasbah.
3. Lunch in the old town.
4. Afternoon at Café Hafa or a taxi to Cap Spartel.
5. Back at the port at least 1 hour before your return ferry [VERIFY recommended check-in time].
