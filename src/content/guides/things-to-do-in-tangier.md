Tangier is small enough to see the highlights in a day, but it rewards a slower visit. Here are the places I'd show a friend.

## In the old town

### The Kasbah
The walled upper part of the medina, with views over the Strait to Spain on a clear day.

<figure>
<img src="/images/tangier-kasbah-walls-port-view.webp" width="1160" height="1600" alt="The Kasbah walls near Bab el Bahr with a view down to Tangier port and the bay" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>The Kasbah walls near Bab el Bahr, looking down on the port. Photo: Tangier Insider</figcaption>
</figure>

**Kasbah Museum (Dar el Makhzen):** open **10:00–18:00**, usually **closed on Tuesdays**. Entry: **30 MAD** (adults), 15 MAD (ages 12–18), 5 MAD (under 12). The official page is not fully consistent about closing days, so check before you go: [Fondation Nationale des Musées](https://fnm.ma/museums/28).

### The medina
Narrow streets, small shops and everyday life. Get a little lost; it's part of the fun. Some alleys are dead ends, so keep an offline map handy (see [Is Tangier safe?](/is-tangier-safe/)).

### Grand Socco and Petit Socco
The Grand Socco is the busy square at the medina's entrance; the Petit Socco is the small café square inside.

<figure>
<img src="/images/petit-socco-night.webp" width="1500" height="1130" alt="Petit Socco square in Tangier at night with lit cafés and a sign pointing to the Kasbah" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Tangier American Legation Museum
A historic building inside the medina with a surprising story: it was the first property the United States owned abroad.

Open **Monday to Friday, 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–17:00** (weekends for groups by appointment). Entry: **20 MAD**, guided tour **50 MAD**. Source: [official website](https://legation.org/about-contact-info).

## Views and cafés

### Café Hafa
A terraced café above the sea, famous for mint tea and sunsets. The sign at the entrance says it was founded in 1921.

<figure>
<img src="/images/cafe-hafa-sign-1921.webp" width="1600" height="1279" alt="Café Hafa entrance sign reading Café Hafa fondé 1921" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<figcaption>Café Hafa, founded in 1921. Photo: Tangier Insider</figcaption>
</figure>

**Local tip:** go in the **morning** if you want it calm and almost empty. For the famous **sunset view**, come in the late afternoon, but expect it to be busy.

### The Marshan
A quiet clifftop neighbourhood near Café Hafa, nice for a walk with sea views.

### Merkala seafront
The coastal road below the Marshan, a favourite place for locals to walk and watch the sunset over the Strait.

The modern marina next to the city port, with yachts, a waterfront promenade and views of the cruise ships in the harbour.

## Outside the city

### Easiest way: the tourist bus
The red [City Tour Tanger](https://tanger.city-tour.com/en/) hop-on hop-off bus goes all the way to **Cap Spartel** and loops around the whole city. According to its official website, a **48-hour ticket** covers **2 routes** and **more than 20 stops**, with an audio guide in 8 languages and free Wi-Fi on board. You can get on and off as many times as you like. Visitors quoted on the official site describe one line covering the old and modern city and another running along the coast, with a stop at the train station: handy if you arrive by train.

Official prices for the **48-hour ticket** (checked November 2026):

<div class="table-scroll">

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adult (15+) | **130 MAD** |

Moroccan citizens get a cheaper "national" ticket (80 MAD for adults). Check the latest prices on the [official website](https://tanger.city-tour.com/en/) before you buy.

### Cap Spartel
The headland where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic, with a lighthouse. Walking around the cape itself is free. The easiest way to get there without a car is the tourist bus above, or a taxi (agree on the price first).

### Caves of Hercules
Sea caves with a famous opening shaped like Africa, near Cap Spartel. There is a small entrance fee; there's no official website with current hours or prices, so check on site.

### Achakar beach
A long Atlantic beach close to the caves, nice for a walk after visiting them.

### Playa Blanca
A beach with white houses below green hills, about **15 km from Tangier** on the coastal road towards **Ksar Sghir**. Without a car, take a **grand taxi** (agree on the price before you get in) or book a ride with the **inDrive** app.
