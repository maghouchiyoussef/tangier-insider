Tangier is small enough to see the highlights in a day, but it rewards a slower visit. Here are the places I'd show a friend.

## In the old town

### The Kasbah
The walled upper part of the medina, with views over the Strait to Spain on a clear day. The Kasbah Museum is here too. [VERIFY opening hours and price] [Youssef: your tip]

### The medina
Narrow streets, small shops and everyday life. Get a little lost; it's part of the fun. [Youssef: your tip]

The Grand Socco is the busy square at the medina's entrance; the Petit Socco is the small café square inside. [Youssef: your tip]

## Outside the city

### Easiest way: the tourist bus
A hop-on hop-off tourist bus goes all the way to **Cap Spartel** and loops around the whole city, so it's a simple way to see the coast and the main sights in one go. Expect **around 150 dirhams** for visitors (Moroccan residents pay less, around 50 dirhams). Prices are a local estimate from November 2026: check at the bus stop before you buy.
